Pricing questions

How is Lookout priced?

By the number of servers (agents) you monitor, billed monthly. Starter and Pro are flat per-server rates; Enterprise is a custom quote that covers volume, on-prem deployment, and compliance needs.

What counts as a “server”?

Any server or VM you install the Lookout agent on. You only pay for hosts that are actively reporting.

Can I deploy Lookout on-prem?

Yes. On-prem deployment — running the control plane inside your own network — is available on the Enterprise plan, so your data never leaves your infrastructure. Talk to sales to scope it.

Is there a free tier?

No. Lookout is a commercial product. Starter is our entry plan; reach out if you’d like to trial it before committing.

Do cheaper plans lose features?

Every plan includes the full monitoring engine — all checks, plugins, the dashboard, and core alerting. Higher plans add scale, advanced alert routing, SSO/RBAC, and deployment options.

Do you offer MSP / partner pricing?

Yes. If you monitor fleets across multiple client organizations, we offer volume pricing and an optional white-label. Mention your client count when you contact sales.
